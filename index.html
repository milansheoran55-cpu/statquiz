How it works

Four steps, about two minutes.

  1. Add your material

    Paste text or upload a .txt file. A chapter or article of 500 to 3000 words works best.

  2. Choose a generator

    The AI generator writes the questions with Gemini. The template baseline fills in blanks from your sentences without AI, so you can compare the two.

  3. Take the quiz

    Answer one question at a time. Nothing is graded until the end.

  4. Read your study plan

    You see your score, the topics you missed most, and the exact passages to revisit.

About this project

Built for Smart India Hackathon 2026, problem statement SIH26101 (MoSPI): an AI learning tool that generates quizzes from uploaded material and recommends what to study.

Generator

Gemini writes questions only from your text, with plausible wrong options and a source sentence.

Recommender

Every question has a topic tag. Wrong answers are counted per topic to find weak areas.

Baseline

A rule-based template generator, used to measure how much better the AI questions are.

What we measure

Question validity rate, distractor plausibility rate and recommendation precision, judged by human reviewers using the downloaded CSV.

Privacy

This site has no server. Your text goes only to Google's Gemini API when you use the AI generator, and your key stays in your browser.
